 intropy sys publish: send the commit time
▎
▎ Atrium now orders a system's topology snapshots by commit time, so a CI job that finishes late no longer rolls the portal back to an older commit. Please add committedAt to the POST /api/topologies body: the committer time of the published commit, in RFC 3339 format, from git show -s --format=%cI <commit>.
▎
▎ - The field is optional. Atrium orders snapshots without it by arrival, as before, so publishing keeps working until the CLI sends it.
▎ - Atrium refuses a time more than an hour in the future (400).
▎ - Responses now say "current": true/false. The CLI could print "stored, but a newer commit is shown" when it's false.
▎ - Two new outcomes worth surfacing:
▎   - 202 with "ignored": the ref isn't main, so nothing was stored.
▎   - 409 Conflict: a component or message name clashes with another system's, and the body says which.
